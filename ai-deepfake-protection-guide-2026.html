WhatsApp New Features in 2026: Smart Message Actions, Events, Scam Alerts and More

By Khan Tech Review | 8–10 min read | Published November 23, 2026

WhatsApp is introducing several useful updates in 2026 to make messaging more convenient and improve account safety. New developments include smart actions for phone numbers and dates shared in chats, improved group planning tools, an updated Events experience, Scam Alert testing and stronger account security.

Some of these features are already rolling out, while others may still be limited to selected users or regions. In this guide, we explain what the updates do, how they can help everyday users and how to check whether they are available on your Android phone or iPhone.

Availability note: WhatsApp releases features gradually. You may not see every feature immediately, even if your app is updated. Availability can depend on your device, account and region.

1. Smart Message Text Actions

WhatsApp is rolling out message text actions that can recognize certain details shared inside conversations. When the app detects a phone number, date, time, address or email address, it may show a dotted underline beneath the recognized text.

Users can tap the highlighted text to see shortcuts related to the information. The feature is designed to reduce the need to manually copy details and switch between different apps.

What can you do with text actions?

  • Phone numbers: Copy a number, save it to contacts or call it using the phone's calling app.
  • Dates and times: Copy the information or use available calendar and event options.
  • Addresses: Copy an address or open it in a compatible maps application.
  • Email addresses: Copy the address or start an email using a supported email app.

The feature has been reported as rolling out to selected Android and iOS users. The available shortcuts may differ depending on the detected text and device.

2. WhatsApp Events Home and Invitations

WhatsApp is expanding its Events experience to make planning group activities easier. In November 2026, Meta announced updates that include an Events Home, customized invitations and options for inviting people who do not have a WhatsApp account.

What is new in WhatsApp Events?

  • Events Home: A central place to manage past and upcoming events.
  • Invitations for non-users: People without WhatsApp can receive an event link, view details and RSVP without downloading the app.
  • Cover photos: Event creators can add a cover photo to invitations.
  • RSVP management: Organizers can keep track of responses.
  • Reminders and plus ones: New options can help organizers coordinate attendance.

These tools can be useful for family gatherings, university events, office meetings and community activities. Meta says the updated Events experience is rolling out over time, so it may not appear on every account yet.

Privacy note: Meta says event invitations are designed to protect phone numbers. Guests who are not in your contacts do not automatically see your phone number, and guests without WhatsApp can see the event address after they RSVP.

3. WhatsApp Group Chat Improvements

WhatsApp has also announced improvements to group conversations. These updates focus on making it easier to organize discussions, get attention when necessary and make decisions with other members.

New group features announced in 2026

  • Improved polls: Group members can use options such as poll end times, hidden voter names and editing a poll question shortly after creating it.
  • @all mentions: Members can use an @all mention to notify the group. In groups with more than 32 people, this option is limited to admins.
  • Create chats from existing groups: Users can start a separate conversation with selected people from an existing group.

These improvements can help families, schools, university groups and workplaces manage busy conversations. Some options may have different availability depending on the group and account.

4. Scam Alert and On-Device Detection

Online scams are a growing concern for messaging-app users. Meta has shared details about Scam Alert, an optional WhatsApp feature that uses an on-device machine-learning model to identify patterns associated with possible scam messages from unknown contacts.

If the feature identifies a likely scam pattern, it can show a warning inside the chat. The user can then decide whether to block the contact, report the conversation or continue chatting.

How does Scam Alert work?

  • The optional feature uses a machine-learning model on the device.
  • It checks messages from non-contacts for patterns associated with scams.
  • A warning can appear if a message is considered suspicious.
  • The user remains in control and chooses what action to take.

Important: Meta described Scam Alert as a limited rollout with Beta availability before a wider release. It is not a guarantee that every scam will be detected, and a warning should not replace careful judgment.

Meta says message content is not automatically sent to its servers for Scam Alert classification. If a user chooses to report a conversation, the reporting process may involve sharing messages with WhatsApp.

5. Stronger Account Security and Passkeys

In August 2026, Meta announced additional WhatsApp security improvements. These include stronger two-step verification, more information about some unknown callers and expanded passkey support.

Stronger two-step verification

Two-step verification adds another layer of protection when someone tries to register your phone number on WhatsApp. Meta announced an upgrade from the traditional six-digit PIN to a longer password that can include letters, numbers and special characters.

Passkeys

Passkeys allow supported devices to verify your identity using a fingerprint, face recognition or your device's screen lock. Meta says users can add more than one passkey when using multiple supported devices.

To check whether the option is available, open WhatsApp and look under Settings, Account and Passkeys. Only set up a passkey on a device you control and protect with a secure screen lock.

More information about unknown callers

Meta also announced additional context for some calls from numbers that are not saved in your contacts. On supported Android devices, this can include whether the number is from another country and whether you share groups with the caller.

This information may help you make a more informed decision before answering, but it cannot prove that a caller is trustworthy.

6. How to Check for New WhatsApp Features

  1. Open Google Play Store on Android or the App Store on iPhone.
  2. Search for WhatsApp Messenger.
  3. Check whether an official update is available.
  4. Install the update from the official app store.
  5. Open WhatsApp Settings and check the relevant account, privacy or group options.
  6. Restart the app if a feature does not appear immediately.

Tip: Updating WhatsApp is recommended, but it does not guarantee immediate access to every new feature. Some updates are enabled gradually by WhatsApp.

7. Privacy and Safety Tips

New features can make messaging easier, but users should continue following basic security practices.

  • Download WhatsApp only from Google Play Store or Apple's App Store.
  • Never share your WhatsApp registration code or two-step verification password.
  • Do not scan QR codes sent by strangers to activate a feature.
  • Review Linked Devices and log out of sessions you do not recognize.
  • Be careful with messages that demand urgent payment or personal information.
  • Use a strong screen lock on your phone.
  • Check official announcements when you are unsure how a new feature handles your data.
