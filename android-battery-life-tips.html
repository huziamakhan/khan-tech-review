Practical Ways to Improve Android Battery Life

By Khan Tech Review | 5 min read | Updated November 3, 2026

Smartphone battery life is important because we use our Android phones throughout the day for communication, browsing, entertainment, work and many other activities. However, some settings and apps can consume more battery than expected.

The good news is that a few practical adjustments can help reduce unnecessary battery consumption. In this guide, Khan Tech Review explains simple steps that Android users can try.

1. Reduce Screen Brightness

The display is one of the major sources of battery consumption on many smartphones. Keeping brightness unnecessarily high can use more power.

  • Reduce brightness when using your phone indoors.
  • Enable adaptive brightness if it works well on your device.
  • Use a shorter screen timeout.

2. Check Battery Usage by Apps

Some apps may continue using battery in the background. Android provides battery usage information that can help you identify apps consuming unusually high power.

  • Open your phone's Settings.
  • Find Battery or Battery Usage.
  • Review which apps are consuming the most power.
  • Restrict background activity for apps that do not need it.
Tip: Settings names can vary depending on your Android version and phone manufacturer.

3. Turn Off Unused Connections

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, hotspot and location services can use power when actively searching for connections or running in the background. Turn off services you are not using when practical.

4. Use Battery Saver Mode

Battery Saver or Power Saving Mode can reduce background activity, notifications and certain performance features to help extend battery life.

You can usually find this option under Settings → Battery. Some phones also allow you to schedule Battery Saver automatically.

5. Keep Your Apps Updated

App updates may include performance improvements and bug fixes. Keeping frequently used apps updated can help avoid problems caused by outdated software.

6. Avoid Unnecessary Background Activity

Too many active apps and background services may contribute to battery drain. Close or restrict apps that you do not need running in the background, but avoid constantly force-stopping every app.

7. Manage Screen Refresh Rate

Some Android phones offer high refresh rate displays such as 90Hz or 120Hz. These settings can make scrolling smoother, but may consume additional power. If battery life is more important than smoothness, try a standard or adaptive refresh-rate setting if available.

8. Follow Sensible Charging Habits

Excessive heat can affect long-term battery health. Use a suitable, reliable charger and avoid exposing your phone to very high temperatures while charging.

  • Use a compatible charger and cable.
  • Avoid leaving your phone in direct sunlight.
  • Use optimized charging features if your phone provides them.

Final Thoughts

Improving Android battery life does not always require a new phone. Reducing screen brightness, reviewing battery usage, managing background activity and using Battery Saver can make a practical difference.

Battery performance varies by phone model, battery age, network conditions and personal usage. Try these adjustments and observe which ones work best on your device.
