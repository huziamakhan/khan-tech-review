7 Battery Saving Hacks Every Android User Should Enable

How-To Guides | Khan Tech Review | 5 min read

Does your Android phone battery run out too quickly? You do not always need a new phone. A few smart settings can help reduce battery consumption and improve daily battery life.

1. Enable Battery Saver

Turn on Battery Saver when your battery is low. It can limit background activity and certain power-consuming features. Find it in Settings → Battery → Battery Saver. Menu names may vary by phone.

2. Reduce Screen Brightness

Your display can use a significant amount of battery. Lower brightness when possible and enable adaptive brightness if it works well for you.

3. Use Dark Mode

On phones with OLED or AMOLED displays, dark mode can help reduce display power consumption. Enable it from Display settings.

4. Restrict Unnecessary Background Apps

Check your battery usage settings to identify apps consuming power in the background. Restrict background activity for apps that do not need constant updates.

5. Reduce Screen Timeout

Set your screen to turn off after 30 seconds or another short interval. This prevents the display from staying on unnecessarily.

6. Turn Off Unused Connections

Switch off Bluetooth, hotspot, GPS or Wi-Fi when you are not using them. Keep in mind that location services may still be needed by important apps.

7. Keep Your Phone Updated

Install official Android and app updates. Updates can include battery optimizations, bug fixes and security improvements.

Final Thoughts

These seven simple changes can help reduce unnecessary battery drain. Results depend on your phone model, battery condition, apps and usage habits. Try the settings that make the biggest difference on your device.
