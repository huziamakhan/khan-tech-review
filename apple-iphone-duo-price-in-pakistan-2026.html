Apple iPhone Duo Price in Pakistan (PTA & Non-PTA), Full Specs & Review (2026)

Published on November 19, 2026 • By Huzaima Khan • Smartphones & Reviews

After years of anticipation, Apple has officially entered the foldable smartphone market with the all-new Apple iPhone Duo. Featuring a passport-style book fold design, a nearly crease-free OLED inner display, and Apple’s flagship A20 Pro chip, the iPhone Duo is easily the biggest smartphone release of late 2026.

For Apple enthusiasts and buyers in Pakistan, the biggest questions right now revolve around its imported price in PKR, PTA approval tax, and everyday durability. Here is a complete breakdown of everything you need to know about the Apple iPhone Duo.

Top Highlights: What Makes the iPhone Duo Special?

1. Wide Passport-Style Design & Invisible Crease

Unlike tall and narrow foldables, the iPhone Duo uses a wider passport-style aspect ratio. When closed, the 5.5-inch cover screen feels compact and comfortable for one-handed typing. When unfolded, the 7.8-inch inner OLED screen acts like a mini iPad—and Apple’s liquid-metal hinge makes the center crease virtually invisible during normal viewing.

2. iOS 20 Multitasking & Apple Intelligence

Powered by the 2nm A20 Pro processor and 12GB of RAM, iOS 20 introduces true desktop-class split-screen multitasking on the iPhone Duo. You can run up to three apps side-by-side, drag and drop files effortlessly, and use on-device Apple Intelligence tools without any lag.

3. Titanium Build & Pro Camera System

Despite housing two screens, the iPhone Duo measures just 4.6mm thin when unfolded thanks to its aerospace-grade titanium chassis. On the back, a triple 48MP camera array delivers flagship photography with 5x optical zoom and ProRes 4K video recording.

Apple iPhone Duo Price in Pakistan (Non-PTA vs. PTA)

With a global starting price of $1,999 for the 256GB base model, the iPhone Duo sits in the ultra-premium category. Here is the estimated pricing breakdown for buyers in Pakistan:

  • Official Global Price: $1,999 (approx. PKR 556,000 before import duties)
  • Expected Market Price in Pakistan (Non-PTA): PKR 585,000 – PKR 620,000
  • Estimated PTA Approval Tax: PKR 175,000 – PKR 210,000 (on CNIC / Passport)
  • Expected Official PTA-Approved Price: PKR 760,000 – PKR 825,000

Note: Local market prices fluctuate depending on early stock availability, USD-to-PKR exchange rates, and storage variants (256GB vs. 512GB/1TB).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How much does the Apple iPhone Duo cost in Pakistan?

The Non-PTA box-pack unit is expected to sell between PKR 585,000 and PKR 620,000 in Pakistan, while a PTA-approved unit will cost around PKR 760,000 to PKR 825,000 after taxes.

2. Does the iPhone Duo have Face ID or Touch ID?

To keep the foldable frame ultra-slim, Apple integrated a fast Touch ID sensor directly into the side power button, which works seamlessly in both folded and unfolded modes.

3. Is the iPhone Duo water-resistant?

Yes, it features an IPX8 water-resistance rating, protecting it against accidental splashes and brief submersion in water.
