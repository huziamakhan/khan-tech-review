Smartphones Buyer's Guide: What to Check Before Buying Under 50k PKR

Before spending your money on a new smartphone in Pakistan, keep these key factors in mind:

  1. Processor Choice: For gaming, look for the MediaTek Helio G99 or Snapdragon 685. Avoid older 12nm processors as they overheat quickly.
  2. RAM & Storage: Aim for at least 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB storage for smooth multitasking and app storage.
  3. Display Quality: Choose Full HD+ (1080p) displays with at least 90Hz or 120Hz refresh rate for smooth scrolling.
  4. PTA Verification: Always buy official PTA-approved devices with a valid brand warranty in Pakistan.
