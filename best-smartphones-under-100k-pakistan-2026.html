Best Smartphones Under 100,000 PKR in Pakistan (2026 Buying Guide)

Published: Nov 17, 2026 · 7 min read · By Huzaima Khan

The under 100,000 PKR price bracket in Pakistan has become one of the most competitive segments in 2026. Buyers no longer have to settle for dim screens or sluggish processors—at this budget, you can realistically expect a 120Hz OLED or AMOLED display, capable main and ultrawide cameras, fast charging, and practical on-device AI tools.

What to Look For Under 100k PKR in 2026

  • PTA Approval & Official Warranty: Always confirm IMEI PTA approval before finalizing a deal. Comparing official box-packed units with non-PTA or patched devices saves headaches later.
  • High-Refresh-Rate AMOLED Display: A 120Hz panel with strong outdoor brightness makes reading, video streaming, and scrolling noticeably smoother.
  • Sustained Performance & Thermals: Look for modern 4nm or 6nm processors with decent heat dissipation so gaming or 4K recording does not throttle quickly.
  • Battery Capacity & Fast Charging: A 5,000mAh+ battery paired with 45W–80W charging keeps daily downtime minimal.
  • Storage & Software Support: Aim for at least 256GB storage and a brand that commits to regular security and OS updates.

Category Breakdown: Which Phone Fits Your Needs?

Buyer Priority Key Hardware Focus Typical Trade-Off

Smart Buying Tips Before You Pay

  1. Verify IMEI via PTA 8484 / DIRBS: Check registration status right in front of the seller before opening the seal or transferring payment.
  2. Compare Official vs. Market Variants: Prices in Pakistan can vary across cities and retailers—check official warranty terms carefully.
  3. Test Camera & Speakers In-Store: Check indoor low-light noise, autofocus speed, and earpiece clarity.
