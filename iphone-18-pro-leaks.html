Apple iPhone 18 Pro Leaks: Variable Aperture Camera, 2nm A20 Pro & Next-Gen Siri AI

HK

Written by Huzaima Khan

Tech Analyst & Editor

Apple’s flagship **iPhone 18 Pro** sets a new benchmark for smartphone innovation. Featuring a hardware-based mechanical variable aperture camera, the revolutionary 2nm A20 Pro silicon, and deeply integrated generative Siri AI, the iPhone 18 Pro is built for power users.

1. World-First Variable Aperture Main Camera

For the first time in iPhone history, Apple introduces a mechanical variable aperture to the 48MP Fusion Main camera. Six laser-cut blades seamlessly adjust between multiple aperture settings (f/1.5 to f/4.0), allowing photographers to control depth of field and exposure with DSLR-grade precision.

  • Main Sensor: 48MP Fusion Camera with f/1.5-f/4.0 mechanical variable aperture & 2nd-gen Sensor-shift OIS
  • Telephoto: 48MP Tetraprism Periscope lens with 4x optical zoom & 3D sensor-shift stabilization
  • Ultrawide: 48MP lens with macro support and 120° field of view

2. 2nm A20 Pro Chip & Apple C2 Custom Modem

Under the hood, the **A20 Pro chip** is manufactured using TSMC’s cutting-edge 2nm process, delivering up to 40% faster performance while drastically improving energy efficiency. Coupled with Apple's custom **C2 Cellular Modem**, energy consumption for 5G data transfer drops by 15% alongside faster upload speeds.

3. Display Brightness & Redesigned Dynamic Island

The 6.3-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display now features an incredible outdoor peak brightness of **3,000 nits** and anti-reflective display coating. The Dynamic Island has also been streamlined to fit up to three active Live Activities simultaneously without sacrificing Face ID performance.

Apple iPhone 18 Pro: Key Features and Improvements

The Apple iPhone 18 Pro brings improvements in camera technology, processing performance, display design and Apple Intelligence. It is designed for users who want a powerful smartphone for photography, gaming, productivity and everyday use.

Apple introduced the iPhone 18 Pro in September 2026. Before purchasing, readers should check Apple's official specifications and confirm local pricing and availability with an authorized seller.

iPhone 18 Pro Camera: Variable Aperture Technology

One of the key camera features is the 48MP Fusion Main camera with variable aperture. Variable aperture allows the camera to adjust how much light enters the lens, helping users adapt to different lighting conditions and photography situations.

For everyday photography, this can be useful when capturing portraits, landscapes, indoor scenes and nighttime moments. Actual results depend on lighting, camera settings and how the photo is captured.

A20 Pro Chip: Performance and Efficiency

The iPhone 18 Pro features Apple's A20 Pro chip, built using 2-nanometer process technology. A newer chip design can help improve performance and power efficiency, although real-world results vary depending on the application and workload.

Users who edit videos, play demanding mobile games, multitask or use advanced smartphone features may benefit from the processing capabilities of a flagship iPhone.

Apple Intelligence and Siri AI Features

Apple continues to develop its Apple Intelligence features and Siri experience. AI-powered tools can help with supported writing, communication and everyday tasks. Feature availability may vary by language, region, device settings and software version.

Buyers should check Apple's official documentation to confirm which Apple Intelligence and Siri features are available in their country and preferred language.

Display and Dynamic Island

The iPhone 18 Pro combines an OLED display with Apple's Dynamic Island interface. The display is designed to provide detailed visuals, rich contrast and a smooth smartphone experience for watching videos, browsing websites and gaming.

The updated Dynamic Island design is another part of the phone's visual experience. Users should consult Apple's specifications for exact display details and supported features.

Battery Life and Charging

Battery life depends on brightness, mobile network conditions, apps, camera use and other settings. Apple states that eSIM-only iPhone 18 Pro models can provide up to 36 hours of video playback under its specified testing conditions.

Apple also advertises charging to approximately 50 percent in around 15 minutes under supported conditions. Actual charging speed can vary with the adapter, cable, temperature and battery level.
