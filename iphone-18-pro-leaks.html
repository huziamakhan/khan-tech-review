Apple iPhone 18 Pro Leaks: Variable Aperture Camera, 2nm A20 Pro & Next-Gen Siri AI

HK

Written by Huzaima Khan

Tech Analyst & Editor

Apple’s flagship **iPhone 18 Pro** sets a new benchmark for smartphone innovation. Featuring a hardware-based mechanical variable aperture camera, the revolutionary 2nm A20 Pro silicon, and deeply integrated generative Siri AI, the iPhone 18 Pro is built for power users.

1. World-First Variable Aperture Main Camera

For the first time in iPhone history, Apple introduces a mechanical variable aperture to the 48MP Fusion Main camera. Six laser-cut blades seamlessly adjust between multiple aperture settings (f/1.5 to f/4.0), allowing photographers to control depth of field and exposure with DSLR-grade precision.

  • Main Sensor: 48MP Fusion Camera with f/1.5-f/4.0 mechanical variable aperture & 2nd-gen Sensor-shift OIS
  • Telephoto: 48MP Tetraprism Periscope lens with 4x optical zoom & 3D sensor-shift stabilization
  • Ultrawide: 48MP lens with macro support and 120° field of view

2. 2nm A20 Pro Chip & Apple C2 Custom Modem

Under the hood, the **A20 Pro chip** is manufactured using TSMC’s cutting-edge 2nm process, delivering up to 40% faster performance while drastically improving energy efficiency. Coupled with Apple's custom **C2 Cellular Modem**, energy consumption for 5G data transfer drops by 15% alongside faster upload speeds.

3. Display Brightness & Redesigned Dynamic Island

The 6.3-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display now features an incredible outdoor peak brightness of **3,000 nits** and anti-reflective display coating. The Dynamic Island has also been streamlined to fit up to three active Live Activities simultaneously without sacrificing Face ID performance.

iPhone 18 Pro Quick Specs

Processor: Apple A20 Pro (2nm Hexa-Core)
RAM & Storage: 12GB RAM, up to 2TB NVMe
Battery & Charging: 4,056 mAh (up to 50% in 15 mins)
OS: iOS 27 with Siri AI & Reference Mode
