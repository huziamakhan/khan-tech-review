iPhone 18 Pro Review: Features, Camera, AI and Buying Guide

Apple's Pro iPhone lineup focuses on performance, photography and premium features. In this guide, we look at the iPhone 18 Pro's announced features, what they could mean in everyday use, and what buyers should consider before spending their money.

By Khan Tech Review Updated: October 27, 2026 Reading time: about 7 minutes

Quick Overview

  • Processor: Apple A20 Pro chip, announced with the iPhone 18 Pro lineup.
  • Main camera: 48MP Fusion Main camera with variable aperture.
  • Display design: Apple announced a smaller Dynamic Island.
  • Software: iOS 27 and Apple's supported intelligence features.
  • Charging: Apple advertises charging to around 50% in approximately 15 minutes under supported wired-charging conditions.
  • Pakistan price: Check current local listings and official availability before buying; prices vary by storage, taxes, warranty and seller.

Specifications and availability can differ by region and model. Confirm important details with Apple before purchasing.

1. iPhone 18 Pro: What's New?

The iPhone 18 Pro is Apple's premium smartphone for people who want a powerful mobile device for photography, video, gaming and everyday productivity. Apple's official announcement highlights changes to the camera system, processor and front display design.

Whether these changes are worth the upgrade depends on the phone you currently own. People using an older iPhone may notice a more meaningful overall improvement than people who already have a recent Pro model.

2. Design and Display

Apple announced a smaller Dynamic Island as part of the iPhone 18 Pro design. The Dynamic Island provides a place for supported alerts, live activities and background tasks while keeping useful information visible.

A premium phone's display is about more than its appearance. Brightness, outdoor readability, colour quality and smooth scrolling all affect the daily experience. If display quality is important to you, compare the exact regional model and its official specifications before deciding.

Is the design a reason to upgrade?

If your current phone is working well, a design change alone may not justify the cost. Consider upgrading when the overall package—camera, performance, battery condition and software support—offers a noticeable benefit for your needs.

3. Camera System and Photography

Camera performance is one of the main reasons people choose an iPhone Pro. Apple lists a 48MP Fusion Main camera with variable aperture for the iPhone 18 Pro. Variable aperture can help the camera adapt to different photographic situations.

For everyday photography, the final image depends on more than megapixels. Lens quality, exposure, focus, image processing, lighting and how steady you hold the phone all matter.

What should you test in the camera?

  • Daylight photos: Look for natural colours, detail and balanced highlights.
  • Night photography: Check whether dark areas retain detail without excessive noise.
  • Portraits: Examine edge detection, skin tones and background blur.
  • Video: Test stabilisation, autofocus and sound in the conditions where you normally record.
  • Zoom: Compare supported zoom levels and image quality rather than relying only on marketing numbers.

If you create YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels or product videos, also test the camera with your preferred social apps. Recording quality and processing can vary between apps and settings.

4. A20 Pro Chip and Performance

Apple announced the A20 Pro chip for the iPhone 18 Pro generation. A newer processor can improve demanding tasks, but real-world performance also depends on software optimisation, temperature, battery condition and the apps you use.

For normal browsing, messaging and social media, most modern premium phones already feel responsive. The biggest practical benefits of a faster chip are more likely to matter during demanding gaming, video editing, computational photography and other intensive workloads.

Gaming and content creation

If gaming is important, look for stable performance over longer sessions, comfortable temperatures and consistent frame rates—not just a short benchmark result. For creators, test how smoothly your editing app handles high-resolution clips and exports.

5. Apple Intelligence and iOS 27

Apple's announcement connects the iPhone 18 Pro lineup with iOS 27 and its supported Apple Intelligence experiences. AI features can assist with selected writing, organisation and other supported tasks, depending on the feature, language, region and software version.

AI availability should not be assumed to be identical for every user. Some features may require a compatible language, internet connection, supported account settings or a software update. Check Apple's current documentation to confirm which functions are available where you live.

Who might benefit from AI features?

  • Students who want help organising notes or working with supported writing tools.
  • Professionals who use compatible tools to manage information and everyday tasks.
  • Content creators who want to explore supported editing and productivity features.

AI features are tools, not a substitute for checking important information. Review generated text and results before using them for school, work or public posts.

6. Battery Life and Charging

Battery life depends on network signal, screen brightness, camera use, gaming, background activity and many other factors. Apple advertises up to 36 hours of video playback for specified eSIM-only models, but this is a controlled-use estimate rather than a promise of the same runtime for every person.

Apple also describes charging to around 50% in approximately 15 minutes under supported wired-charging conditions. Actual charging speed can vary with the adapter, cable, temperature, battery level and settings. Check the official requirements for the exact model.

Battery tips for everyday use

  • Use automatic brightness or lower the screen brightness when practical.
  • Keep your software updated.
  • Review apps that use substantial background activity.
  • Use compatible, reputable charging accessories.
  • Check battery health if you are buying a used iPhone.

7. Storage and Choosing the Right Model

Choose storage based on how you use your phone. Photos, 4K videos, offline downloads, games and editing projects can consume space quickly. Cloud storage can help with some files, but it does not remove every need for local storage.

Before buying, confirm the available storage options for your region directly from Apple or an authorised seller. Do not rely on unverified listings or assume that every capacity is available in every market.

8. iPhone 18 Pro Price in Pakistan

The price in Pakistan can differ according to storage capacity, import status, applicable taxes, seller margin, warranty and availability. Currency movements can also change local prices.

We recommend checking current prices from reputable retailers and confirming whether a device is officially supported, what warranty is included, and whether any relevant registration or tax costs apply. Avoid making a purchase decision based on an old price or a social media rumour.

  • Ask for the exact model number and storage capacity.
  • Confirm the warranty terms in writing.
  • Check whether the device is new, used or refurbished.
  • Compare the final payable price, not just the advertised starting price.

9. Should You Upgrade to the iPhone 18 Pro?

The iPhone 18 Pro may be worth considering if you need a premium camera system, high performance and Apple's current software features. The best choice depends on your budget and the device you currently use.

It may be a good fit if you:

  • Regularly record photos and videos for work or social media.
  • Use demanding apps or mobile games.
  • Want the features available in Apple's current Pro lineup.
  • Can comfortably afford the phone without stretching your budget.

You may want to wait if you:

  • Already own a recent iPhone Pro that meets your needs.
  • Mostly use messaging, browsing and basic apps.
  • Would need to compromise on essentials to afford the upgrade.
  • Prefer to wait for more independent reviews and local price comparisons.

Apple lists a 48MP Fusion Main camera with variable aperture. Check the official technical specifications for the complete camera system.

Does the iPhone 18 Pro support AI features?

Apple announced iOS 27 and supported Apple Intelligence experiences for this generation. Availability can depend on language, region, software and feature requirements.

Local prices can vary by storage, taxes, warranty and seller. Check current reputable listings and confirm the total cost before purchasing.

Final Verdict

The iPhone 18 Pro brings together Apple's announced camera, chip, design and software updates in a premium smartphone. Its value will depend on which features matter most to you, how your current phone performs and the price available in your market.

Before buying, confirm specifications and availability using Apple's official information, compare trusted local prices, and look for independent real-world reviews. That is a safer way to decide than relying on rumours or unverified specifications.
