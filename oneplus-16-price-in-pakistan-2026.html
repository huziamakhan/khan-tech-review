OnePlus 16 Price in Pakistan, Launch Date & Full Specs: The 9,000mAh Battery Flagship

Published on November 19, 2026 • By Huzaima Khan • Smartphones & Reviews

OnePlus is shaking up the smartphone industry once again with the upcoming OnePlus 16. Following the massive battery trend of late 2026, industry reports indicate the OnePlus 16 will pack an extraordinary 9,000mAh silicon-carbon battery, an ultra-smooth 165Hz OLED display, and Qualcomm’s next-generation Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chipset.

For power users, mobile gamers, and buyers in Pakistan looking for an all-day flagship that eliminates battery anxiety, the OnePlus 16 is shaping up to be one of the most anticipated releases of the season. Here is everything we know so far about its specifications, expected price in Pakistan, and PTA status.

Top Highlights: Why Everyone Is Searching for OnePlus 16

1. Unmatched 9,000mAh Battery Life

While most flagship phones still stick to 5,000mAh or 6,000mAh cells, OnePlus is pushing boundaries with a 9,000mAh battery. Thanks to advanced silicon-carbon battery technology, the phone remains remarkably slim despite holding nearly double the capacity of traditional flagships. Expect 2 to 3 days of moderate use or over 12 hours of continuous PUBG Mobile and Call of Duty gaming on a single charge.

2. Next-Gen Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 & 165Hz Display

Powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6, the OnePlus 16 is built for extreme multitasking, on-device AI processing, and console-level mobile gaming. Paired with a 165Hz LTPO OLED panel, scrolling and high-frame-rate gaming feel noticeably smoother than standard 120Hz screens while dynamically dropping to 1Hz to save power.

3. Pro-Grade Triple 50MP Camera Setup

OnePlus continues to refine its flagship camera system with a versatile triple-lens setup:

  • 50MP Sony LYT-series Primary Sensor with Optical Image Stabilization (OIS) for crisp low-light shots.
  • 50MP Periscope Telephoto Lens offering 3.5x lossless optical zoom and up to 120x digital zoom.
  • 50MP Ultra-Wide Lens with macro capabilities for landscape and group photography.

OnePlus 16 Expected Price in Pakistan (Non-PTA vs. PTA)

Based on global pricing leaks (starting around $799 to $849 internationally), here is the estimated pricing breakdown for Pakistani buyers:

  • Expected Global Price: $799 (approx. PKR 222,000 – 230,000 before taxes)
  • Expected Box-Pack Price in Pakistan (Non-PTA): PKR 235,000 – PKR 255,000
  • Estimated PTA Approval Tax: PKR 115,000 – PKR 135,000 (on CNIC / Passport)
  • Expected Official PTA-Approved Price: PKR 350,000 – PKR 385,000

Note: Official local pricing may vary depending on import duties, USD-to-PKR exchange rates, and storage variants.

Should You Wait for the OnePlus 16?

If you prioritize battery endurance, ultra-fast 120W charging, and top-tier gaming performance, waiting for the OnePlus 16 makes complete sense. Compared to competitors like the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra or iPhone 18 Pro Max, the OnePlus 16 offers nearly twice the battery capacity at a significantly lower starting price.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the battery capacity of the OnePlus 16?

The OnePlus 16 is expected to feature a massive 9,000mAh silicon-carbon battery with 120W wired and 50W wireless fast charging.

2. Will the OnePlus 16 launch officially in Pakistan?

While OnePlus does not have direct official retail stores in Pakistan, imported box-pack units (both Non-PTA and PTA-approved) are widely available through major mobile markets and online retailers across Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, and Bahawalpur shortly after the global launch.

3. Which processor powers the OnePlus 16?

It is powered by the flagship Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 processor paired with up to 24GB RAM.
