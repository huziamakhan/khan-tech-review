PTA Mobile Tax Check Karne Ka Tarika (2026 Complete Guide)

Nov 13, 2026  ·  6 min read  ·  By Huzaima Khan

Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) ke rules ke mutabiq, agar aap koi bhi foreign imported ya overseas se laya gaya smartphone Pakistan mein istemal karna chahte hain, toh usay PTA se register karwana lazmi hai. Agar registration nahi karwai jati, toh 60 din ke baad sim card mobile par kaam karna band kar deta hai. Is article mein hum aapko tafseel se batayenge ke aap apne mobile ka PTA tax aur status kaise check kar sakte hain.

PTA Mobile Tax Check Karne ke 3 Asan Tareeqay

Aap apne mobile ka tax aur status darj zail asan tareeqon se check kar sakte hain:

1. USSD Code (*8484#) ke Zariye (Sab se Asan Tareeqa)

  • Apne mobile ka IMEI number maloom karne ke liye dial karein: *#06#. (IMEI 1 note kar lein).
  • Apne phone se direct dial karein: *8484#.
  • Screen par diye gaye options mein se mobile registration ya tax inquiry select karein.
  • Apna 15 digit ka IMEI number enter karein.
  • Kuch seconds mein aapko SMS par mobile ka PTA tax aur approval status mil jaye ga.

2. DIRBS Official Website ke Zariye

  • PTA ki official DIRBS website (dirbs.pta.gov.pk) par jayein.
  • Website par diye gaye search box mein apna 15 digit ka IMEI number enter karein.
  • Captcha verification mukammal karke search par click karein.
  • Aapko phone ka model, status (Approved/Non-Approved) aur estimated custom duty / tax amount screen par show ho jaye gi.

3. SMS Service ke Zariye

  • Apne mobile ke messages box mein jayein.
  • Apna 15 digit ka IMEI number type karein.
  • Is message ko 8484 par send kar dein.
  • Kuch hi der mein PTA ki taraf se tax aur status ki tafseelat SMS ke zariye موصول ho jayengi.

PTA Tax Calculate Kaise Hota hai? (Passport vs CNIC)

Pakistan mein mobile tax do buniyadi bunyado par calculate hota hai:

  • Passport par Registration: Agar aap overseas Pakistani hain aur apne passport par phone register karwate hain, toh government ki taraf se tax par kuch concession (chhoot) milti hai.
  • CNIC par Registration: Agar aap local ID card (CNIC) par phone register karwate hain, toh standard commercial aur custom duties lagti hain jo phone ki international market value ke hisab se hoti hain.

PTA Tax Ada Karne ka Tarika

Jab aapko tax ki exact amount pata chal jaye, toh aap in steps ke zariye tax pay kar sakte hain:

  • *8484# ya official website se apna PSID tracking code hasil karein.
  • Apne kisi bhi Pakistani bank ki mobile banking app (maslan EasyPaisa, JazzCash, HBL, Meezan Bank waghera) mein login karein.
  • "Govt Payments", "Tax Payment" ya "FBR-DIRBS" section select karein.
  • Apna PSID code enter karein aur tax amount confirm karke pay kar dein. Tax pay hone ke kuch ghanton ke andar phone PTA approved ho jata hai.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Kya bina tax diye phone chal sakta hai?
Jawab: Haan, aap Wi-Fi par internet aur baqi apps chala sakte hain, lekin 60 din ke baad cellular network (calls aur mobile data) block ho jaye ga.

Q2: IMEI number kahan se milta hai?
Jawab: Aap apne phone ke dial pad par *#06# dial karke apna IMEI number easily hasil kar sakte hain.
