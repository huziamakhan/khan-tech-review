PTA Mobile Tax Check Karne Ka Tarika (2026 Complete Guide)

November 13, 2026 · Updated November 18, 2026 · 7 min read · By Huzaima Khan

Pakistan mein imported ya overseas se laye gaye smartphones ko local mobile networks par use karne ke liye applicable registration requirements poori karna zaroori ho sakta hai. Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) ka Device Identification, Registration and Blocking System (DIRBS) mobile devices ke IMEI status ko manage karta hai.

Is guide mein hum batayenge ke aap apne mobile ka IMEI number kaise check kar sakte hain, PTA registration kaise karte hain, passport aur CNIC options ka kya farq hai aur FBR ka PSID payment process kaise kaam karta hai.

Important: PTA device registration system ko manage karta hai, jabke mobile devices par applicable customs duties aur taxes Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) ke rules ke mutabiq hote hain. Exact amount device aur application details ke hisaab se DIRBS/FBR system se confirm karein.

1. Mobile ka IMEI Number Kaise Check Karein?

PTA status check karne se pehle apne smartphone ka IMEI number maloom karein. IMEI ek unique identification number hota hai.

  1. Apne mobile ka dial pad open karein.
  2. *#06# dial karein.
  3. Screen par IMEI number ya multiple IMEI numbers show honge.
  4. Har IMEI number ko note kar lein.

Dual-SIM aur eSIM phones mein ek se zyada IMEI numbers ho sakte hain. Registration karte waqt device ke tamam relevant IMEIs check karein.

2. PTA Mobile Status SMS ke Zariye Check Karein

IMEI status check karne ke liye ye simple method use kar sakte hain:

  1. Mobile ke Messages app mein jayein.
  2. Apna 15-digit IMEI number message mein type karein.
  3. Message 8484 par send karein.
  4. PTA/DIRBS ki taraf se milne wale reply ko read karein.

Reply mein device ki compliance ya registration status ki information mil sakti hai. Agar result unclear ho, official DIRBS portal se dobara verify karein.

3. DIRBS Official Website se PTA Registration

PTA ka Device Registration System (DRS), DIRBS process ka hissa hai. Imported phone ki registration ke liye official portal use karein.

  1. Official PTA DIRBS Registration Portal open karein.
  2. Required account ya login process complete karein.
  3. Apni applicable applicant category select karein.
  4. CNIC ya passport ki required details enter karein.
  5. Phone ke tamam relevant IMEI numbers enter karein.
  6. Application submit karke system ka response check karein.

System application details verify karne ke baad applicable duty aur tax calculate kar sakta hai. Agar payment required ho, to aapko Payment Slip ID (PSID) generate ya receive hogi.

4. Passport aur CNIC Registration Mein Kya Farq Hai?

DIRBS application mein applicant ki situation ke mutabiq passport ya CNIC ki details maangi ja sakti hain.

Passport Registration

  • International travellers ke liye applicable process available hai.
  • Passport aur travel details ki verification required ho sakti hai.
  • Passport category ke liye applicable eligibility conditions follow karein.

CNIC Registration

  • Pakistan ke local applicants ke liye CNIC details use hoti hain.
  • Applicant ki identity aur device information verify ki jati hai.
  • Applicable tax system ke calculation ke mutabiq hota hai.
Passport aur CNIC ke tax amounts phone ke model, assessed value, applicable rules aur applicant eligibility ke mutabiq different ho sakte hain. Kisi fixed discount ya amount ko assume na karein. Final amount official FBR/DIRBS system se confirm karein.

5. PTA Mobile Tax Kaise Pay Karein?

Agar DIRBS application ke baad FBR system PSID issue karta hai, to us payment slip par di gayi instructions follow karein.

  1. Apna generated PSID aur payable amount carefully check karein.
  2. PSID ki validity aur payment deadline verify karein.
  3. FBR/DIRBS ki taraf se bataye gaye authorized payment channel ka use karein.
  4. Payment receipt ya transaction confirmation save karein.
  5. Payment confirmation ke baad apna IMEI status dobara check karein.

Payment channels aur available options waqt ke saath change ho sakte hain. Is liye sirf woh payment method use karein jo aapke official PSID ya government instructions mein diya gaya ho.

Fraud se bachain: Kisi unknown agent ko apna CNIC, passport details, OTP ya payment information na dein. Unofficial PTA tax calculators ko final government bill na samjhein.

6. 60 Days ka PTA Registration Rule

FBR ki published DIRBS guidance ke mutabiq, imported mobile device mein local SIM use hone ke baad registration requirements poori na karne par 60 din ke baad device local networks par block ho sakta hai. Is rule ka application device aur user category ke mutabiq verify karna zaroori hai.

International visitors jo foreign SIM par roaming use karte hain, unke liye separate conditions ho sakti hain. Local SIM use karne par unki situation different ho sakti hai. Travel ya temporary registration ke liye official DIRBS guidance check karein.

7. Official PTA aur FBR Resources

Latest rules, application process aur payment details ke liye hamesha government sources ko priority dein.

  • PTA DIRBS Device Registration Portal
  • FBR Mobile Devices Regularization – DIRBS
  • Pakistan Telecommunication Authority
  • Federal Board of Revenue

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. PTA mobile tax online kaise check kar sakte hain?

Official DIRBS registration portal par application details submit karein. Agar FBR system PSID issue karta hai, to us par applicable payable amount check kar sakte hain.

Q2. IMEI number kaise maloom hota hai?

Mobile dial pad par *#06# dial karein. Screen par IMEI number show ho jayega.

Q3. Kya 8484 par IMEI bhej kar phone ka status check ho sakta hai?

Ji haan, apna 15-digit IMEI 8484 par SMS karke device status ki information hasil kar sakte hain.

Q4. Kya PTA tax har smartphone ke liye same hota hai?

Nahi. Applicable duty aur taxes device ki assessed value, model aur registration category ke mutabiq different ho sakte hain.

Q5. PSID kya hota hai?

PSID ka matlab Payment Slip ID hai. Ye FBR payment process mein generate hone wali identification hoti hai, jiske zariye applicable government payment ki jati hai.

Q6. Tax payment ke baad phone kaise verify karein?

Payment confirmation ke baad apne IMEI ko 8484 ya official DIRBS system ke zariye dobara check karein.

Conclusion

PTA mobile registration aur tax check karna imported smartphone users ke liye important hai. IMEI status check karein, official DIRBS portal se application submit karein aur FBR ki taraf se issue hone wale PSID aur payment instructions follow karein.

Kisi bhi payment se pehle device details aur payable amount carefully verify karein. Latest information ke liye official PTA aur FBR websites ko consult karna behtareen tareeqa hai.

Quick Checklist
  • IMEI number check karein.
  • 8484 par device status verify karein.
  • Official DIRBS portal use karein.
  • PSID aur payable amount verify karein.
  • Authorized payment channel use karein.
  • Payment ke baad IMEI status dobara check karein.
