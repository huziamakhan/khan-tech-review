PTA Mobile Registration & Tax Check Guide in Pakistan (2026)

Nov 14, 2026  ·  6 min read  ·  By Huzaima Khan

Pakistan mein imported ya overseas se laye gaye smartphones par PTA tax pay karna aur unhein register karwana har mobile user ke liye zaroori hai. Agar aap apna phone legal banana chahte hain, toh yeh guide aapko poora process samjhaye gi.

1. Mobile ka IMEI Number Kaise Check Karein?

Apne phone ka IMEI number pata karne ke liye apne dialer se ***#06#* dial karein. Dono SIM slots ke IMEI numbers note kar lein kyunki registration ke doran inki zaroorat parti hai.

2. PTA Tax Check Karne ke Tareeqe

Aap apna tax two methods se check kar sakte hain:

  • USSD Code: Apne phone se `*8484#` dial karein aur instructions follow karein.
  • Official Portal: PTA ki official DIRBS website par ja kar IMEI enter karein.

3. Online Registration Process

FBR/PTA portal par account banakar CNIC aur Passport details ke zariye request submit ki jati hai, jahan se PSID number milta hai jiske zariye 1-link ya bank app se tax pay hota hai.

4. Conclusion

Hamesha tax pay karne se pehle current slabs zaroor check kar lein taake unexpected charges se bacha ja sake.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Kya Passport par tax kam lagta hai?
Jawab: Ji haan, overseas Pakistanis ke liye passport par register hone wale phones par tax rates CNIC ke muqable mein kam hote hain (jab shart poori ho).

Q2: Tax pay karne ke baad signal kab chalte hain?
Jawab: Payment verify hone ke baad aam tor par 24 ghante ke andar device ke signals on ho jate hain.
