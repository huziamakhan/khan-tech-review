Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra Review: Specs, Camera and Galaxy AI

The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra combines a high-resolution camera system, a large AMOLED display and Galaxy AI features. This guide explains its officially published specifications and the features buyers should consider before purchasing.

Editorial note: This is a specifications-based review, not a hands-on device test. Real-world performance depends on usage, software and network conditions.

1. Display and Design

Samsung lists a 6.9-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with an adaptive refresh rate of up to 120Hz. The large screen is designed for reading, video playback, gaming and multitasking.

The phone measures approximately 7.9mm thick and weighs 214g, according to Samsung's published specifications. Its size and weight are worth considering if you prefer compact phones.

2. Camera System

The rear camera system includes a 200MP wide camera, a 50MP ultra-wide camera, a 50MP telephoto camera and a 10MP telephoto camera. The front camera is 12MP.

  • 200MP main camera with an f/1.4 aperture.
  • 50MP ultra-wide camera with an f/1.9 aperture.
  • 50MP telephoto camera with 5x optical zoom.
  • 10MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom.
  • 12MP front camera for selfies and video calls.

Camera quality also depends on lighting, processing and the photographer. Megapixel counts alone cannot establish which phone takes the best photos. Independent camera tests are useful when comparing devices.

3. Processor and Galaxy AI

The Galaxy S26 Ultra uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy processor. Samsung also highlights Galaxy AI tools for supported tasks such as photo editing and everyday assistance.

Available AI functions may vary by language, region, software version and account requirements. Check local availability before buying the phone for a particular feature.

4. Battery and Charging

Samsung lists a typical battery capacity of 5,000mAh. Actual battery life varies with screen brightness, gaming, camera use, mobile signal and other settings.

Charging speed and supported charging features should be checked against the official specifications for your region. Do not assume that advertised maximum speeds apply in every situation.

5. Key Specifications

  • Display: 6.9-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X
  • Refresh rate: Adaptive up to 120Hz
  • Main camera: 200MP
  • Ultra-wide camera: 50MP
  • Telephoto cameras: 50MP and 10MP
  • Front camera: 12MP
  • Processor: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy
  • Battery: 5,000mAh typical capacity
  • Weight: 214g

Specifications, memory configurations and availability may vary by market. Confirm details with Samsung before purchasing.

6. Who Should Consider This Phone?

The Galaxy S26 Ultra may be worth considering if you want a large display, versatile camera hardware, advanced smartphone features and Samsung's Galaxy AI tools.

If price, one-handed use or lightweight design matters most to you, compare other phones before deciding. We have not independently tested battery endurance, gaming performance or camera quality for this article.

7. Price and Availability in Pakistan

Prices and stock can change between retailers and over time. We are not publishing an unverified local price. Check the official Samsung Pakistan specifications and local authorised retailers for current availability, warranty and pricing.

Final Verdict

On paper, the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra offers a large 120Hz display, a high-resolution multi-camera system, a 5,000mAh battery and Galaxy AI features. Whether it is the right choice depends on your budget and priorities. Compare verified local prices and independent reviews before buying.
