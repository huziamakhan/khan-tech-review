How to Transfer Data from iPhone to Android

How-To Guides | Khan Tech Review | 5 min read

Switching from an iPhone to an Android phone does not mean you have to leave all your important data behind. You can transfer many of your contacts, photos, videos and other information during Android setup.

1. Prepare Both Phones

Charge both phones, connect them to Wi-Fi and update them if possible. Keep your iPhone unlocked and have your Apple Account details available when needed.

2. Use a Cable for Data Transfer

During the setup of your new Android phone, choose the option to copy apps and data from an old device. Connect your iPhone to Android using a compatible cable and follow the instructions displayed on the Android screen.

3. Transfer Photos and Videos

Select photos and videos when the transfer options appear. If you use iCloud Photos, check that your original photos are available and follow the transfer instructions supported by your Android device.

4. Transfer Your Contacts

Select contacts during the setup transfer. You can also use your Google Account to sync contacts if they are stored in a supported account.

5. Move WhatsApp Chats

WhatsApp offers an official chat transfer process for supported iPhone-to-Android transfers. Follow the instructions shown in WhatsApp and during Android setup. Requirements can vary by device.

6. Check Your Important Apps

Some iPhone apps have Android versions, while others may not. Download your apps from Google Play and sign in to restore data where the app supports account-based backup.

7. Turn Off iMessage

After switching, deregister iMessage for your phone number so that messages from iPhone users can arrive as regular SMS or supported messaging services on your Android phone.

Final Thoughts

Moving from iPhone to Android can be straightforward when you prepare both devices and follow the official transfer instructions. Transfer options depend on your Android model, software version and the data you want to move.
