WhatsApp New Features in 2026: Smart Message Actions, Scam Alerts and More

By Khan Tech Review | 7–9 min read | Published November 23, 2026

WhatsApp continues to add tools that make everyday messaging more useful and safer. Recent 2026 updates include smart actions for information shared in chats, new group event features and additional account-protection tools. Some features are rolling out gradually, while others are still limited to selected beta users.

In this guide, we explain the most useful WhatsApp updates reported or announced in 2026, what they do, how to check whether they are available on your phone, and what users in Pakistan and elsewhere should know before relying on them.

Availability note: WhatsApp releases features in stages. A feature mentioned in this article may not appear on every Android or iPhone yet. Do not install unofficial APK files or join unknown links to force an update.

1. Smart Message Text Actions

One of the newer WhatsApp improvements reported in November 2026 is the ability to recognize certain types of information inside a message and offer quick actions. When WhatsApp identifies a phone number, date, time, address or email address, it may underline the text with a dotted line. Tapping the highlighted text can show relevant shortcuts.

What can these shortcuts do?

  • Phone numbers: Copy the number, add it to contacts or place a call using your phone's calling app.
  • Dates and times: Copy the details or create a calendar event, depending on the available options.
  • Addresses: Copy the address or open it in a compatible maps application.
  • Email addresses: Use the available option to copy the address or start an email.

This can save time when someone shares an appointment, location or contact detail in a conversation. The feature has been reported as rolling out on Android and iOS, but the exact options may differ by device and app version.

2. WhatsApp Events and Group Planning

WhatsApp has continued improving group conversations with event-related tools. Events can help a group organize a gathering, meeting or other shared activity without relying on a long chain of messages.

Depending on the group and version, event tools can let members see event details and respond to an invitation. WhatsApp announced further event improvements in November 2026, so users should check the app's group options to see which tools have reached their account.

When are Events useful?

  • Planning a family dinner or get-together.
  • Coordinating a school, university or community meeting.
  • Sharing a date and time with members of a group.
  • Keeping event information easier to find than scattered chat messages.

3. Scam Alert: WhatsApp's On-Device AI Scam Detection

Scam messages are a serious problem for messaging-app users. Meta has described an optional WhatsApp Scam Alert feature that uses a machine-learning model on the device to identify patterns associated with possible scam messages from unknown contacts.

If the feature detects a likely scam pattern, it can show a warning to the user. The user can then decide whether to block, report or continue the conversation. Meta says the message classification takes place on the device and message content is not automatically sent to WhatsApp or another party for this check.

Important: Scam Alert has been described as a limited or gradual rollout, including beta availability. Do not assume it is active on every account. A warning system can also make mistakes, so always use your own judgment.

Even if Scam Alert is not available on your phone, you can still protect yourself by avoiding suspicious links, refusing to share verification codes and checking unexpected money requests through another trusted channel.

4. Stronger Account Security and Passkeys

Meta announced additional WhatsApp account-security improvements in August 2026. These include a stronger password option for two-step verification and more context about some calls from unknown numbers on supported Android devices.

Stronger two-step verification

Two-step verification adds a second layer of protection when your phone number is registered with WhatsApp. Meta says it is upgrading the traditional six-digit PIN to a longer password that can include letters, numbers and special characters. The exact setup screen may vary during the rollout.

Passkeys

Passkeys let supported devices verify your identity using a fingerprint, face recognition or device screen lock. To check availability, open WhatsApp Settings and look under Account for Passkeys. Only set up a passkey on a device you control and protect with a secure screen lock.

More information about unknown callers

Meta also described additional context for some calls from numbers that are not saved in your contacts, such as country information or shared groups on supported Android devices. This information may help you make a more informed decision before answering, but it cannot prove that a caller is trustworthy.

5. Other WhatsApp Updates to Watch

WhatsApp's official newsroom has also highlighted updates to group chats, calling and other everyday messaging features during 2026. Availability and rollout timing can differ by region, operating system and account.

  • Group chat improvements: WhatsApp has announced changes intended to make group conversations easier to manage.
  • Calling updates: New ways to invite people to calls and use WhatsApp calling have been announced during the year.
  • Username development: WhatsApp has discussed username-related changes, but users should rely on in-app settings and official announcements for confirmed availability.

Because features can change after testing, avoid treating screenshots or social-media posts as proof that a tool is available to everyone.

6. How to Check for New WhatsApp Features

  1. Open Google Play Store on Android or the App Store on iPhone.
  2. Search for WhatsApp Messenger.
  3. If an official update is available, review the app listing and install it.
  4. Open WhatsApp Settings and check the relevant account, privacy or group options.
  5. Restart the app if a newly installed feature does not appear immediately.

Tip: Feature availability can be controlled by WhatsApp's rollout schedule. Updating the app is sensible, but it does not guarantee that every new feature will appear immediately.

7. Privacy and Safety Tips Before Using New Features

  • Download WhatsApp only from Google Play or Apple's App Store.
  • Never share your SMS registration code, two-step verification password or passkey recovery details.
  • Do not scan QR codes sent by strangers to “activate” a feature.
  • Review Linked Devices and log out of sessions you do not recognize.
  • Be careful with messages that demand urgent payment or personal information.
  • Keep your phone's operating system updated and use a strong screen lock.
  • Check official WhatsApp announcements when a feature's privacy behavior is unclear.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are the latest WhatsApp features in 2026?

Recent updates and announcements include smart message text actions, group event improvements, Scam Alert testing, stronger two-step verification and passkey support. Not every feature is available to all users.

2. Is WhatsApp Scam Alert available to everyone?

No. Meta described Scam Alert as an optional feature with a limited rollout. Availability may depend on your account, device and app version.

3. How do I get the latest WhatsApp update?

Use Google Play Store on Android or Apple's App Store on iPhone. Avoid unofficial downloads and websites claiming to unlock hidden features.

4. Does WhatsApp smart text recognition read all my private messages on a server?

Reporting about the text-actions feature says recognition is performed on the device. For the exact privacy details of any feature, consult WhatsApp's official announcements and privacy information.

5. Why can't I see a new WhatsApp feature?

WhatsApp often releases features gradually. Your app may be updated while the feature is still unavailable for your account or region.

6. Are WhatsApp passkeys safer than verification codes?

Passkeys can provide strong, phishing-resistant sign-in on supported devices, but you should still secure your phone and recovery methods.

Final Thoughts

WhatsApp's 2026 updates focus on making conversations more convenient and improving protection against scams and account takeovers. Smart message actions and group events can save time, while Scam Alert, stronger verification and passkeys add useful security options as they become available.

Keep WhatsApp updated through official app stores, check your settings from time to time and remember that no automated warning can replace careful judgment. At Khan Tech Review, we aim to explain technology updates in clear, practical language so readers can make informed decisions.
