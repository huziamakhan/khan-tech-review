Xiaomi 18 Pro Max Design and Display

The Xiaomi 18 Pro Max features a large 6.9-inch display designed for watching videos, browsing, gaming and productivity. Its LTPO AMOLED technology supports an adaptive refresh rate from 1Hz to 120Hz.

The display also includes Xiaomi Privacy Display technology, which helps reduce screen visibility from side angles. This can provide additional privacy when using the phone in public places.

Another standout feature is the 2.9-inch AMOLED rear display. It is integrated into the camera area and can show notifications, support selected mini-app experiences and work as a viewfinder for rear-camera selfies.

  • 6.9-inch main LTPO AMOLED display
  • 120Hz adaptive refresh rate
  • 2624 × 1208 resolution
  • Up to 4000 nits multi-scenario peak brightness
  • 2.9-inch rear AMOLED display
  • Xiaomi Privacy Display technology
  • Dolby Vision and HDR10+ support

Xiaomi 18 Pro Max Camera: Dual 200MP Leica System

Camera performance is one of the biggest highlights of the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max. Xiaomi has partnered with Leica to develop a camera system featuring high-resolution sensors and Leica Summilux optical technology.

200MP Main Camera

The main camera uses a 200MP sensor with a large 1/1.28-inch Light Fusion 960L image sensor and LOFIC HDR technology. Its f/1.67 aperture and optical image stabilization are designed to help capture detailed photographs in different lighting conditions.

200MP Telephoto Camera

The second 200MP camera is a 75mm-equivalent telephoto lens. It includes optical image stabilization and is designed for zoom photography, portraits and distant subjects.

50MP Ultra-Wide Camera

The 50MP ultra-wide camera offers a 102-degree field of view. It can be useful for landscape photography, architecture and group photographs.

Front Camera

For selfies and video calls, the phone includes a 50MP autofocus front camera with support for high-resolution video recording.

The combination of two 200MP cameras makes this device interesting for mobile photography enthusiasts. Actual image quality will still depend on image processing, lighting conditions and camera testing.

Processor and Performance

The Xiaomi 18 Pro Max is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 processor, manufactured using a 2nm process according to the official specifications.

The chipset is designed to handle demanding applications, multitasking, advanced graphics and AI-related workloads. The phone also includes Xiaomi's 3D IceLoop cooling system to help manage heat during intensive use.

  • Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6
  • 2nm manufacturing process
  • Adreno GPU
  • Qualcomm AI Engine
  • Xiaomi 3D IceLoop cooling system
  • LPDDR5X RAM
  • UFS 4.1 storage

For gaming enthusiasts, the combination of a flagship chipset, high-refresh-rate display and advanced cooling hardware makes this phone worth watching. Independent gaming and thermal tests are needed to judge its real-world performance.

Battery and Charging

Battery capacity is another major attraction of the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max. The global model includes a 7050mAh typical-capacity silicon-carbon battery.

The phone supports 100W wired HyperCharge and 50W wireless HyperCharge. These charging options provide flexibility for users who prefer either wired or wireless charging.
